# **App Name**: SafeUG

## Core Features:

- Local Guide Connection: Connect foreign tourists with certified local guides for navigation, cultural insights, and safety guidance.
- One-Touch SOS & Emergency Services: Provide a prominent one-touch SOS button for immediate contact with emergency services and access to local emergency contact information.
- Real-time Location Tracking & Sharing: Enable GPS tracking of tourists within safe boundaries, geofencing for alerts, and sharing location with designated emergency contacts.
- Contextual Safety Tips & Alerts: Deliver critical safety tips for various situations and provide real-time travel alerts (weather, security concerns) tailored to the user's location.
- AI-Powered Language Translator: Offer an AI tool for translating common phrases and questions into local languages to facilitate communication with locals.
- Community Safety Reporting: Allow local communities to report suspicious activities, animal sightings, and other safety concerns to improve overall security.
- Emergency Contacts Management: Allow users to securely save and easily access their personal emergency contacts within the app.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A confident and trustworthy blue with a hint of green, suggesting safety, natural beauty, and clear skies (#2E7EBF). In HSL, this is approximately H=200, S=60%, L=45%.
- Background color: A soft, cool light off-white (#ECF4F9), visibly of the same hue as the primary color, for a clean and refreshing canvas. In HSL, this is approximately H=200, S=15%, L=95%.
- Accent color: A vibrant and energetic aqua-green (#2EDEB3), providing a bright contrast and emphasizing interactive elements. In HSL, this is approximately H=170, S=80%, L=60%.
- Headlines: 'Alegreya' (serif) for an elegant, intellectual, yet contemporary feel that invites reading.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for modern clarity, optimal readability across various screen sizes, and a neutral, objective presentation of information.
- Utilize clear and intuitive line-art icons that are easily recognizable, with subtle design elements that could reflect local Ugandan cultural patterns or motifs, particularly for features related to guides or community. Use filled icons for active states to ensure quick visual feedback.
- Adopt a mobile-first, clean, and uncluttered card-based layout. Key safety features like the SOS button and guide connection should be prominently displayed for quick access. Information should be organized logically with clear visual hierarchy, utilizing ample white space for readability and ease of navigation.
- Implement subtle, smooth transition animations for screen navigation, component loading, and interactive elements. Animations should enhance user experience without being distracting, such as gentle fades for content updates and satisfying bounces for button presses, to convey a polished and responsive interface.
